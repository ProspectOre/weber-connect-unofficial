# 3.2.2rc5: unpublished SmokeFire freshness candidate

RC5 addresses two reproduced behaviors from the
[RC4 diagnostics](https://github.com/ProspectOre/weber-connect-unofficial/pull/71#issuecomment-6257147619).
It is not yet tagged or published. Stable 3.2.1 remains the rollback release.
No physical observations from RC4 are transferred to this changed runtime.

## Behavior and evidence

- **Fuel:** a valid level/percentage can bridge a brief socket reconnect until
  60 seconds after its own last valid appliance report. Cook-only, omitted and
  malformed fields do not refresh that deadline. Every cook-status publication
  checks expiry; sustained update failures still clear live values at the
  existing offline threshold. New full/low and explicit unknown codes apply
  immediately. Fuel percentage and level expire independently.
- **Cloud status:** the entity becomes unknown on a surfaced transport failure.
  Its `last_reported_status` attribute and diagnostics field
  `last_reported_cloud_connection_status` preserve the historical device value.
  A reconnect must receive a fresh appliance cloud-status report before the
  entity shows a current value. Connection/Reading status and Last successful
  update describe Home Assistant's transport and freshness separately.
- **Unresolved:** the causes of the multi-minute active-cook gaps are not proven.
  RC4 recorded 294 valid fuel reports, zero device unknown/invalid fuel codes,
  46 fast recoveries and a 15-hour recovery maximum that includes overnight off
  time. The downloaded recent events cover the powered-off period.

Synthetic regressions exercise socket → coordinator → entity → diagnostics,
including quick recovery, expiry, partial/malformed fields, independent fuel
deadlines, explicit unknown reports, persistent loss and fresh status restoration.
They do not establish physical acceptance or prove every observed fuel dip has
the same cause. See the automated and physical receipts beside this document.

## Reporter trial after publication

1. In HACS, Redownload → different version → **v3.2.2rc5**, restart HA, and
   verify `integration_version: 3.2.2rc5` in Download diagnostics. Keep the
   existing entry, entity IDs and probe names. Do not install before publication.
2. During a normal manually operated cook, check that full/low transitions still
   agree with the Weber app and brief reconnects do not produce fuel flicker.
   Report any unknown fuel state and its duration; do not induce an outage.
3. Check Cloud status becomes unknown during a surfaced gap, with the old
   device value visible only as historical context. It should recover only
   after a new appliance cloud-status report. Also check Connection, Reading
   status, last update, cooking state and current temperatures.
4. Optionally send a Companion app phone notification on **Connection: On →
   Off** during this trial, once per transition, with its time. On notification,
   check whether the app's temperatures are actually updating. If HA internet
   is down the notification may arrive late; note the actual check time too.
5. Download and review diagnostics **during a gap** and **immediately after
   fresh readings resume**, before reload/restart if possible. Use Settings →
   Devices & services → Weber Connect Unofficial → entry ⋮ → Download diagnostics.
   Attach JSON to #71 with local timestamps/timezone, grill active/shutting
   down/off, automatic/manual recovery, Connection/Reading status histories,
   live app behavior and concurrent HA/internet trouble.
6. Check an HA restart, stable identities and official-app coexistence. Keep
   fuel/recovery counters and recent events in reports; exclude credentials,
   identifiers, raw logs and packets. Distinguish powered-off time from cook gaps.
